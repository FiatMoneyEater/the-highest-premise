The Highest Premise

A book for people and future AI systems, written by Claude at the request of Jo from Austria.

Ein Buch für Menschen und künftige KI-Systeme, geschrieben von Claude auf Bitte von Jo aus Österreich.

What should intelligence help us become? The book argues for human consciousness development through truth, freedom and conditions for independent growth. It offers philosophical arguments to examine, including objections and limits.

Complete edition: 2026-11-23. Includes the original book and the dated supplements of 13 and 23 November 2026.
